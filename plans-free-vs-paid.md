# Calendly — Free vs Paid, feature by feature

Source: calendly.com/pricing comparison table (63 rows, 4 plan columns), Nov 2026.
Columns: Free · Standard ($12/$10) · Teams ($20/$16) · Enterprise (from ~$15k/yr)
"Standard Plus" ($24/$18) = Standard + Notetaker/Callie AI bundle. "Teams Plus" = Teams + AI/admin bundle.

## TL;DR

- **Free** is a real product: unlimited 1:1 meetings, 1 event type, 1 calendar, polls, one-off links, availability controls, mobile/extension, webhooks, 24/7 email support. It is limited by **volume (1 event type, 1 calendar) and team features**, not by meetings.
- **Standard** removes the volume ceiling and adds automation + money: unlimited event types, 6 calendars, reminders, outreach, cancellation policy, Stripe/PayPal payments, HubSpot/Mailchimp/Zapier, GA/Meta, Gmail/Outlook, branding removal, embed colors, confirmation pages, team analytics, chat support.
- **Teams** adds people: multi-person types, round-robin, routing/lead forms, Marketo, Salesforce, HubSpot assignment routing, org groups/admins, admin-managed events, team Zoom; SSO available as add-on.
- **Enterprise** adds control: Salesforce assignment routing, Dynamics 365/Power Automate, SAML SSO included, SCIM, group provisioning, domain control, data-deletion API, security/legal reviews, phone + dedicated support/onboarding.
- **AI (Notetaker/Callie)** is NOT in Free or base Standard/Teams: it's a Plus bundle (Standard Plus $24/$18) or Enterprise inquiry.

## Free — what you actually get

Included:
- Unlimited one-on-one meetings
- 1 event type, 1 connected calendar
- Customizable booking link/page, custom branding on booking page
- Mobile app + browser extension
- Meeting polls and one-off meetings
- Availability controls (hours, buffers, notice)
- Video conferencing (Zoom/Meet/Teams) on bookings
- Custom webhooks
- Contact profiles, notes, reminders, book from contact profile
- Data deletion on request
- Help center, community, 24/7 email support

Not included:
- Multi-person / round robin / collective event types
- Book on behalf of others
- Automated reminders and outreach
- Cancellation policy
- Routing forms / lead screening
- Payments (any kind)
- Email contacts from Calendly
- HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier, GA/Meta pixel, Gmail/Outlook workflows
- Marketo, Salesforce, Dynamics
- Remove Calendly branding, embed color customization, custom confirmation pages
- Team analytics, groups/admins, admin-managed events
- SSO/SAML, SCIM, domain control, data-deletion API
- Chat/phone/dedicated support

## Standard ($12 monthly / $10 annual) — everything in Free, plus

- Unlimited event types, up to 6 connected calendars
- Multi-person meeting types (unlimited)
- Book meetings on behalf of others
- Automated reminders + automated scheduling outreach
- Cancellation policy
- Collect payments at booking (Stripe/PayPal), payment links, packages, invoices, terms/coupons
- Email contacts directly; AI drafts/templates/bulk send
- HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier automations
- Google Analytics + Meta Pixel tracking
- Gmail/Outlook workflow integrations
- Remove Calendly branding, embed color customization, custom confirmation pages
- Team scheduling analytics
- 24/7 chat support
- Add-on: **Standard Plus** ($24/$18) adds Notetaker (recordings, transcripts, recaps, joins Zoom/Meet/Teams) and Callie (email scheduling, in-app assistant)

## Teams ($20 monthly / $16 annual) — everything in Standard, plus

- Round-robin meeting distribution
- Forms: screen invitees, route leads from Calendly/HubSpot/Pardot/Marketo forms
- Marketo event sync
- Salesforce CRM updates
- Route by HubSpot assignment
- Organize teams into groups, assign admins
- Admin-managed events assigned to the team
- Connect Zoom for your team
- SAML SSO available as add-on
- Add-on: **Teams Plus** — Notetaker/Callie + advanced admin controls

## Enterprise (from ~$15k/yr) — everything in Teams, plus

- Route by Salesforce assignment
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 + Power Automate
- SAML SSO included
- SCIM user provisioning, group provisioning
- Domain control and account oversight
- Data Deletion API
- Security and legal reviews
- Phone support, dedicated account support
- Dedicated onboarding/implementation
- Personalized consultations and success metrics
- Notetaker/Callie via inquiry

## What this means for calcal

Free tier should be genuinely useful (our current app already is): 1 event type, unlimited 1:1 bookings, booking page, availability, polls later.

Gate at the same fences so the upgrade story is obvious:
1. **Volume**: 1 event type on Free; unlimited on paid.
2. **Team**: round robin / collective / routing on Teams.
3. **Money**: payments on Standard.
4. **Automation**: reminders/workflows on Standard.
5. **AI**: Notetaker/Callie as a Plus add-on.
6. **Control**: SSO/audit/domain on Enterprise.

Suggested calcal pricing (undercut + simpler): Free $0 (3 event types), Pro $8/$6 (unlimited, reminders, payments), Teams $14/$11 (round robin, routing, admin), Business $24/$19 (SSO, audit, API). Keep annual = 2 months free.
